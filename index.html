About

I’m Inna. I help businesses look as good as they really are.

For 12 years I’ve run my own photography brand, innaguba.com. I built the site, wrote the copy and took the photos, and it still brings me clients today. Photography taught me patience and an eye for detail, and I bring the same care to your brand.

In practice, that means a website, logo and photos that show clearly what you do, build trust at first glance and make it easy for the right clients to get in touch. And when you need a change later, you know exactly who to ask.

Process

From first sketch to a brand people remember.

  1. 1. Get in touch Tell me about your business and what you need. I reply within two working days. You get a clear plan and a fixed price.
  2. 2. Design and build Structure, words, photos and design, made for your clients. You see the work at agreed stages and give feedback. No surprises at the end.
  3. 3. Launch I check every detail, then your website goes live and you get all the files. Everything ready to use, and fully yours.
  4. 4. Care after launch I don’t disappear once we’re done. Updates, small changes and advice whenever you need them. You get someone who knows your site inside out.

Questions

What can you do for my business?

Everything your brand needs to look good online: a website, a logo and visual identity, and photos, videos and posts for social media. All from one person, so it fits together.

Where do you work?

Anywhere. Websites and branding work fully online, so it doesn’t matter where you are. For photo and video shoots, I’m happy to come to you.

How much does it cost?

You get a fixed price before we start, based on what you need. No hidden costs.

A website usually takes 4–6 weeks; a logo, a photo shoot or a video 2–3 weeks. You get a timeline at the start.

In English, Czech, Ukrainian or Russian, whichever suits you best. I prefer writing things down, so everything we agree is on record.

A deposit at the start and the rest on delivery.
