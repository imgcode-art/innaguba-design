  • To run this website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest).

I keep enquiries for as long as we are in contact and for up to 3 years afterwards. Invoices and accounting records are kept for 10 years, as required by Czech law. Then the data is deleted.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools. Fonts are loaded from this website itself, not from third-party servers.

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to data portability and to object to processing. Just write to me at innaguba@seznam.cz.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in the Czech Republic the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz), in Austria the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at).
