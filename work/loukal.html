Brand photo and video · Sustainable fashion label, Czech Republic

Clothes made for moving

Photos and short videos for LOUKAL, a Czech label of sustainable women’s clothing. Its designer, Anastázie Jegorová, creates small collections with timeless cuts, and every piece is sewn to order in the Czech Republic from fabrics that are kind to nature.

RolePhotography, video, direction on set
ClientLOUKAL, sustainable women’s fashion, Czech Republic
DateThree shoots, 2024–2026
DeliveredCampaign photos, detail shots and short videos

The client

Slow fashion, sewn to order

They design

Simple, timeless cuts that flatter a real body and leave room to move, including pieces for pregnancy.

They make

Each piece is sewn to order by seamstresses in Czech workshops, in your size and colour. No warehouse of clothes nobody bought.

They choose

TENCEL Lyocell, Lenzing EcoVero viscose, organic cotton, recycled waterproof fabric and wool: materials picked to do as little harm as possible.

They sell

Through the loukal.eu e-shop and at markets and in shops, where women can try the small collections on before they order.

The brand promise in three words: freedom, movement and joy. The photos had to show all three.

The idea

Don’t pose. Move.

A still pose shows a dress. Movement shows how it feels to wear it: how the fabric falls, swings and follows the body. So instead of standing the women in a row, I asked them to walk, turn, dance and laugh, and caught the moments in between.

When you are busy moving, you forget the camera, and the smiles are real.

Warm light

Warm colours in morning sun

Rust, plum and caramel fabrics, a flat with tall windows and soft light on the wooden floor. When the sun comes in low, the colours of the clothes glow on their own, with no extra lighting needed.

Details

The quality is in the details

People who buy sustainable clothing want to know what they are paying for. Close-ups of buttons, ties, seams and the small leaf on the label tell that story better than any product description, and they work well in the e-shop and on Instagram.

Light and white

Clothes for real life

For the lighter pieces we chose a bright white room. A little girl, a fluffy cat and a bunch of flowers joined in, because that is where these clothes will really be worn: at home, with the people and animals you love.

And the clean full-length shots the e-shop needs

Video

Short films for social media

For other collections we took the clothes out into the streets. Short clips of walking, turning and smiling at the camera are made for Instagram and Facebook, where a few seconds of movement stop the scroll better than a still photo.

Where the photos live

One look, everywhere

Anastázie uses the photos wherever LOUKAL meets its customers, so the brand looks the same online and in the shop.

Website and e-shop

Product pages, collection banners and the story of the brand on loukal.eu.

Campaigns

Launches of new collections and seasonal campaigns.

Instagram

Posts, stories and the short videos made for movement on screen.

Design stores

In-store presentation at PLACE STORE in Brno and Tvorba Store in Prague, showrooms of Czech designers.

Three shoots in three years. When a client keeps coming back, it is the best review a photographer can get.

What this shows

Photos that feel like wearing it

Across three shoots, LOUKAL got everything a small fashion label needs: campaign images with a mood, close-ups that prove the quality, clean shots for the e-shop and short videos for social media. All in one consistent style, so the brand looks the same wherever customers meet it.
